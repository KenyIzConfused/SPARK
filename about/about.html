Welcome to S.P.A.R.K. - Sign and Play: ASL retention for Kids

Our Mission

Our mission is to make ASL learning fun and accessible for children.

Our Story

S.P.A.R.K. was founded with the goal of making ASL learning engaging and effective for young learners, specifically focusing on the neurodivergent community attending the solano Solano East Central School Integrated SPED Center

Our Team

Our team consists of dedicated college students in Nueva Vizcaya State University whom are passionate about making ASL learning accessible and enjoyable for children.
